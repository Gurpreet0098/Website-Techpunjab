FAQ

Questions business owners ask us

What exactly is a managed IT service provider (MSP)?

An MSP is your outsourced IT department. Instead of calling someone only when things break, you pay a fixed monthly fee and we look after your computers, network, email, backups and security continuously — fixing problems and preventing them.

We already have a local computer technician. Why switch?

A technician fixes things after they break. We prevent breakdowns, keep records of every device and password, run tested backups and protect your business against fraud. You can keep your technician for small jobs — we're happy to work alongside them.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is a fixed monthly fee based on the number of computers and users, and the plan you choose. After the free IT health check we give you a written quote — no surprise bills.

Do you visit our office?

Yes. Most issues are solved remotely, and our engineers come on-site whenever hands-on work is needed. See the list of cities above.

Do you support Tally and GST billing software?

Yes. We back up Tally data daily, can host Tally on the cloud for access from multiple locations, and keep billing PCs and printers running.

Are we locked into a long contract?

No. Our agreements are simple and flexible, and if you ever decide to leave, we hand over all passwords, records and documentation.
